# Deck Style

- Canvas: 1920x1080, white background
- Visual tone: rigorous academic rebuttal deck, not product marketing
- Accent color: deep blue `#2563EB`
- Secondary accent: slate `#1E293B`
- Highlight color for warnings or discarded routes: muted red `#B91C1C`
- Typography: clean sans-serif, high contrast, strong slide titles
- Layout: generous margins, large central content block, minimal decoration
- Footer: thin blue bar with small white text `Strict Reproduction of SCD on Mistral Pairs`
- Charts and diagrams: flat design, no glossy effects, no gradients, no drop shadows
- Figures from the paper, if used: keep the original content exactly as-is and only add framing, title, and footer
- Overall tone: conservative, precise, evidence-first
